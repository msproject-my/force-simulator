Formula Reference & Force Theory

1. X-Axis Component ($F_x$)
$F_x = F \cdot \cos(\theta)$

+X = Right  |  -X = Left

2. Y-Axis Component ($F_y$)
$F_y = F \cdot \sin(\theta)$

+Y = Upward  |  -Y = Downward

3. Resultant Force ($F_R$)
$F_R = \sqrt{(\sum F_x)^2 + (\sum F_y)^2}$

Vector Pythagorean Theorem
