Select Your Interactive Force Simulator

This interactive simulator suite is designed to help you master force component resolution (Fx, Fy), forces in equilibrium, quadrant analysis, Center of Gravity determination (Method 1 & 2), multi-vector resultant force calculations (FR), and beam reaction forces (FA, FB).

Forces in Equilibrium

Simulate horizontal (Fx) and vertical (Fy) forces acting on an object to analyze balanced (Fnet = 0) conditions.

  • Equilibrium axis lines (Fnet = 0)
  • Real-time motion response
  • Auto-Balance one-click option
LAUNCH SIMULATOR 1

Single Force Resolution

Resolve a single force F at angle (𝝷) into horizontal (Fx) and vertical (Fy) components across Quadrants I-IV.

  • Fx = F · cos(𝝷) & Fy = F · sin(𝝷)
  • Inward / Outward Vector direction
  • Interactive Drag & Touch Angle
LAUNCH SIMULATOR 2

Multi-Vector Resultant

Combine concurrent forces to determine Resultant Force Magnitude (FR) and Direction Angle (𝝷) using Component Tables.

  • Up to 8 concurrent forces
  • Component Sum Breakdown
LAUNCH SIMULATOR 3

Method 1: Principle Moment of Force

Determine Center of Gravity (x) using Principle of Moment of Force (Σ MCCW = Σ MCW) relative to Pivot O.

  • Σ MCCW = Σ MCW relative to Fulcrum O
  • Upward, downward & angled loads (θ)
  • Auto-snap Fulcrum O to calculated CoG
  • Step-by-step algebraic working expansion
LAUNCH SIMULATOR 4

Method 2 – Resultant Moment of Force

Determine Center of Gravity (x) using Resultant Moment of Force (Σ MA = (Σ F1+F2+...Fn⊥) · x) relative to Reference Point A.

  • Resultant Moment = (Σ F⊥) · x
  • Flexible Reference Point A (Left or Right End)
  • Perpendicular force (F⊥ = F sinθ) resolver
  • Step-by-step ratio & equation solver
LAUNCH SIMULATOR 5

Two Supports Beam

Calculate reaction forces at Support A (FA) and Support B (FB) using vertical equilibrium and moment equations.

  • Adjustable Support A & B positions
  • Σ Fup = Σ Fdown & Σ MA = 0
  • Upward, downward & angled loads
  • Step-by-step exam working derivation
